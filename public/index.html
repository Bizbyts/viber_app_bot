How to Connect Your Real Viber Bot (When Ready)

Whenever you want to link a real Viber chatbot account, follow these 3 simple steps:

1
Get your Free Viber Bot Auth Token

Visit partners.viber.com, log in with your phone number, click Create Bot Account, and copy your Authentication Token.

2
Add Environment Variables on Netlify

In your Netlify Site dashboard, navigate to Site configuration ➔ Environment variables, and add:

VIBER_AUTH_TOKEN=your_token_from_partners_viber_com
3
Your Webhook URL

Netlify redirects /webhook straight to your serverless function:

https://your-site.netlify.app/webhook
